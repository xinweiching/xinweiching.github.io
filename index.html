Core Beliefs

  • Own Your Understanding — AI is a powerful accelerant, but outsourcing comprehension is how technical debt accumulates silently. Engineers who truly understand their systems, not just their outputs, are the ones who can actually fix them.
  • Engineers Are Bridges — Technology only matters when it reaches people. The real-world work of translating between human needs and technical systems isn't something AI can substitute for as it requires judgment, context, and accountability.
  • Hardware Grounds Software — Working at the silicon level keeps one honest about what computation actually costs. Every abstraction leaks eventually; knowing what's underneath makes one a better engineer at every layer.
  • Open Source as a Responsibility — The tools that matter most are built in the open. Giving back to the community is part of the deal.
