Core Beliefs

  • Architecting the Execution — AI handles the execution, but I own the architecture. My role as an engineer has evolved from writing syntax to designing constraints: defining explicit requirements, mapping domain logic, choosing the right stack, and enforcing architectural boundaries. By offloading the generation, I focus my energy where it matters most: bridging the gap between the product owner's vision and structural integrity through rapid, intentional iteration.
  • Engineers Are Bridges — I see engineering as a bridge between people and technology. Building software is only part of the work; understanding human needs and translating them into technical solutions is where real impact happens.
  • Hardware Grounds Software — Understanding the silicon beneath the abstraction keeps an engineer honest about what computation actually costs. Every layer leaks eventually; knowing what lies underneath makes better decisions possible.
  • The Spirit of Open Source — I have a deep appreciation for the open-source community. I love studying open codebases to learn best practices.
