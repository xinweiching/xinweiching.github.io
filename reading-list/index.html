Machine Learning: The High-Interest Credit Card of Technical Debt Sculley et al.  ·  2015
📄 Link

A seminal Google research paper highlighting how ML systems accumulate technical debt at a much faster rate than traditional software. It explores system-level risks like entanglement, hidden feedback loops, undeclared consumers, and data pipeline jungles, introducing patterns to identify and mitigate these costly maintenance burdens.

This was one of the reading assignments in my MLOps class. It perfectly captures the messy, chaotic reality of the field, yet reminds me why I find this work so deeply fascinating.
